# AI-use statement: a template

Most universities now ask doctoral candidates to disclose how they used generative AI. Commons logs every model
call (table `model_calls` in `data/commons.db`: date, component, role, provider, model, where it ran, the most
sensitive tier of material it saw, and the sources retrieved), so the statement can be specific. Check your
institution's policy and adapt the wording with your supervisors.

To summarise the log:

```bash
sqlite3 data/commons.db "SELECT role, model, locality, max_tier, COUNT(*) FROM model_calls GROUP BY 1,2,3,4"
```

## Template

> I used a locally run research system, Commons (version X), to manage my sources and to retrieve evidence. Its
> models ran [on my own computer / on <named university service>]. [No interview or participant data was
> processed by any external service.]
>
> The system used language models for four tasks: extracting candidate claims from sources, each with a verbatim
> quote that was checked mechanically against the source text; screening newly published work for relevance;
> proposing links between claims and my propositions, all of which I reviewed and confirmed or rejected; and
> retrieving and summarising passages in answer to my questions, with citations to the passages used.
>
> The models were [author model], [worker model] and [verifier model, from a different model family]. A full
> log of model calls is retained.
>
> No text generated by these models appears in this thesis. All arguments, interpretation and writing are my own.
> Every source cited was read by me.

Change the last paragraph if it is not true for you. It should be.
